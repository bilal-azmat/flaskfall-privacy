Flaskfall Privacy Policy

Flaskfall for Android · Effective 12 November 2026

In short

  • Flaskfall has no accounts, and your progress stays on your device. We have no servers and cannot see it.
  • The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device information to show those ads, as explained below.
  • If you are in a region where consent is required, you choose whether ads can be personalised, and you can change your choice later.

This policy explains what information Flaskfall handles and what choices you have. "We" and "us" means Bilal Azmat, the developer of Flaskfall.

1. Your game progress

Flaskfall saves your progress so you can carry on playing. This includes the stars and best tilt counts for each lab, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your sound, vibration and tilt-control settings. It is stored inside the app on your device and is not sent to us.

You do not need to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other contact details in the game.

Android may include this saved data in your device's own backup if you have backups turned on. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, and we cannot access it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Flaskfall shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Some are optional: you can choose to watch an ad to see a hint for the lab you are playing. Others are full-screen ads that may appear when you move on to the next lab.

To show ads, measure them and help prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID and other identifiers;
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location may be worked out;
  • device and app details, such as the device model, operating system and language;
  • how you interact with ads, including ads viewed and tapped, and crash and performance information.

Google, and where you allow it its advertising partners, may use this information to show ads that are personalised to you. Android's advertising features, such as Topics, may also be used to choose ads. Without consent where it is required, only non-personalised or limited ads are requested. These can still use some device information for purposes such as frequency limits, fraud prevention and measurement.

We do not receive information that identifies you personally from Google. We see only overall reports, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

You can read more in Google's Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Your ad choices

  • Europe and the UK: if you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can review or change your choice at any time from the pause menu under Privacy choices.
  • US states with privacy laws: if you live in one of these states, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data from the pause menu under Privacy choices.
  • Your device: you can delete or reset your advertising ID, and manage ad topics and related features, in your Android settings. On many devices these are under Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Google: you can manage personalised ads at My Ad Center.

4. Fonts and settings checks

Flaskfall downloads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time it runs, and reads a small settings file hosted on GitHub Pages that controls how ads run. These are normal web requests, so Google and GitHub receive your IP address. They do not include your game progress.

5. Phone permissions and sensors

Flaskfall uses internet access, network status and the advertising ID, mainly for the ads and requests described above, and vibration for haptic feedback. If tilt control is on, the game reads your phone's motion sensor (accelerometer) to know which way is down. These readings are used only on your device, while you play, and are never stored or sent anywhere. The game does not ask for access to your precise location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling Flaskfall removes it. A device backup may keep a copy until that backup is replaced or deleted. Because we do not receive your progress, we cannot restore it for you.

Information collected for ads is kept under Google's own policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe and the UK, or US state laws such as California's), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to or opt out of certain uses, including targeted advertising.

We do not hold personal information about you on our own systems, except what you send us if you contact us. You can use the ad choices in section 3, and Google's tools for information it collects. We do not sell your information for money, but the sharing described in section 2 may count as "selling" or "sharing" under some US state laws. Your Android settings and the choices above let you limit it.

8. Children

Flaskfall is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
